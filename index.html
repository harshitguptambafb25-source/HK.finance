METHODOLOGY

Calculation Integrity

The application follows a simple principle: never manufacture data. If an input does not exist, the dependent calculation is shown as unavailable.

01. Normalize

Uploaded labels are matched against an editable alias dictionary. You can modify the dictionary to match your own Excel terminology.

02. Validate

Missing periods, missing metrics, balance-sheet relationships and consistency checks are surfaced before analysis.

03. Calculate

Ratios and valuation outputs use explicit formulas and protected division. No denominator means no fabricated result.
